A. Requirements 
In this project, you will work in groups to design, implement, and evaluate an AI-powered chatbot 
tailored for a specific application domain (e.g., healthcare, education, customer service, finance, 
travel, etc.). The requirements for your chatbot are outlined below: 
Basic Features (3 marks out of 10 marks in Final Demo)  
Your chatbot should be capable of understanding user queries, providing relevant 
information, and performing essential tasks within the selected domain. Specifically: 
• The chatbot must be able to communicate effectively within the defined domain of 
application. 
• It should handle typical user queries and provide relevant responses in a ch osen 
language (e.g. English or Chinese).  
Additional Features (7 out of 10 marks in Final Demo) 
Beyond the basic requirements, you are expected to incorporate additional features to 
enhance your chatbot. You have the flexibility to select any features you prefer, such as (but 
not limited to): 
• Voice recognition and synthesis 
• 2D/3D avatar integration 
• Fine-tuned large language model (LLM) 
• User-friendly interface 
Innovation and technical complexity in your chosen features will be considered in the 
evaluation and may positively impact your marks.

B. Group Formation 
You are required to form groups of 2-4 students. The workload should be equally shared among all 
group members, and as such, each student in the group will receive the same project mark. 
C. Project Development  
       Final Demo and Presentation  [10 marks] 
Presentation 
 
Clearly state all the features you have implemented, including both the basic and additional 
features.  
Additionally, provide a detailed description of how the additional features were implemented 
or integrated into your chatbot. Highlight any technical challenges  you encountered and how 
you addressed them. Explain their capabilities as well as any limitations or challenges 
encountered during development. 
 
Live Demonstration 
You are required to conduct a live demonstration of your developed chatbot. Please design a 
scenario or specific use case that effectively showcases the capabilities of your chatbot within 
the chosen domain.  
For example, you may interact with your chatbot using domain-specific queries to demonstrate 
its ability to provide accurate responses based on your knowledge base or vector database. If 
you have implemented features such as speech recognition, consider including a verbal 
conversation to highlight this functionality.  
If applicable, please also summarize any evaluation results and user feedback during your 
demonstration.
